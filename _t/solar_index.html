Why solar

Three good reasons

Lower monthly bills

Power you make on your own roof is power you do not buy from the grid.

Plenty of sunshine

Karachi gets strong sun for much of the year, which suits rooftop solar well.

Low upkeep

Panels have no moving parts. Occasional cleaning and a check-up are usually all they need.

FAQ

Common questions

What size system do I need?

It depends mainly on your monthly electricity use and your available roof space. Use the calculator above for a first idea, then a free site survey confirms the right size for your property.

How much does a solar system cost?

Cost depends on system size, equipment and installation needs. The prices on this demo page are examples only. After a survey you receive a written quote for your exact system.

Will my solar system work during load-shedding or a power cut?

A standard grid-tied system without batteries switches off during a grid outage for safety reasons. If you want backup power, ask us about hybrid systems with batteries, which cost more.

What is net metering?

Net metering lets you send extra solar power to the grid and receive credit for it on your bill. It requires approval from the electricity utility, and rules and timelines can change, so we confirm the current process with you.

Is the free site survey really free?

In this demo, yes: the survey is presented as a free, no-obligation visit to check your roof and bills before you decide anything.
